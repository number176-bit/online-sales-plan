# Online Sales Plan

Planning repository for online product sales, especially Japan-to-Korea product sourcing and smart store operation.

## Purpose

This repository organizes product research, listing drafts, pricing, sales channels, operation checklists, and marketing ideas.

## Main Goal

Build a repeatable online sales flow:

1. Find product candidates
2. Check demand, price, margin, and risk
3. Create product listing content
4. Sell through online channels
5. Review results and improve the next product

## Repository Structure

| Folder | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `product-research/` | Product candidate research and sourcing notes |
| `listing/` | Product titles, descriptions, and detail page drafts |
| `operation/` | Order, shipping, CS, and inventory checklists |
| `marketing/` | YouTube, blog, shorts, and SNS promotion ideas |

## Initial Sales Channels

| Channel | Purpose | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smart Store | Main sales channel | High |
| YouTube | Trust building and product introduction | High |
| Blog / Cafe | Search traffic and product explanation | Medium |
| Instagram / Shorts | Short-form product exposure | Medium |
